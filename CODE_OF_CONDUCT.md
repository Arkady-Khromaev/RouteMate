# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to making participation in RouteMate a welcoming and respectful experience for everyone, regardless of background, experience level, or identity.

## Our Standards

Examples of positive behavior include:

- Being respectful and considerate.
- Welcoming different opinions and perspectives.
- Providing constructive feedback.
- Focusing on what is best for the community.
- Helping others learn and improve.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, and disrespectful communication.

## Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards.

Contributors are expected to follow this Code of Conduct when participating in Issues, Pull Requests, discussions, and other project activities.

## Reporting Issues

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please contact the project maintainers through GitHub.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all project spaces, including the repository, Issues, Pull Requests, and community discussions.
